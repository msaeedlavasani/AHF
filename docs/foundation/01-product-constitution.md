# Product Constitution

**Status: RESOLVED**  
**Version: v0**

## Canonical product definition

> Apex Home Fit is a personalized home-training coach that creates an appropriate program for each user and accompanies the user step-by-step through the real execution of training.

## Canonical product loop

```text
Assess
→ Prescribe
→ Perform
→ Observe
→ Adapt
```

## Foundation principles

### 1. Personalized

Training originates from the user's needs and conditions rather than from a fixed universal workout.

### 2. Prescription != Execution

What the user is prescribed to do is separate from how the system guides, observes, measures, or assists execution.

### 3. Intelligent capabilities are enhancements, not prerequisites

Camera, pose tracking, AI, sensing, or similar intelligent capabilities may improve the workout experience but must not be prerequisites for the workout to remain executable.

### 4. AHF accompanies execution

AHF is not merely a program generator. Its product responsibility continues through actual workout execution.

### 5. Adaptation is a distinct responsibility

Prescription, execution, and adaptation are separate responsibilities.

Past observations and outcomes may inform future adaptation without rewriting the meaning of past prescriptions or executions.
